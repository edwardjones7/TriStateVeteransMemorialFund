Mike was raised in Philadelphia and South Jersey and built a 30-year career as an IRS agent, rising to Supervisory Branch Chief — followed by years as a director at KPMG and as a CPA.

A 4th-Degree member of the Knights of Columbus, Mike joined the Advisory Board of the TriState Veterans Memorial Fund to serve as a resource for veterans who suffer from service-related issues and other conditions.
