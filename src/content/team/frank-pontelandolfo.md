Born in Camden and raised in Mantua Township, Frank graduated from Texas A&M University and joined the Air Force in 1969, earning his pilot wings and flying tactical airlift missions throughout Southeast Asia.

Frank accrued over 500 hours of combat time and rose to become Chief of Staff for the Delaware Air National Guard, retiring as a Brigadier General. He is a frequent community speaker on veterans' issues, including PTSD and moral injury.
