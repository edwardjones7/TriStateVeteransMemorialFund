Jeffrey has been a resident of Washington Township since 2001. He holds a degree in Criminal Justice from Camden County College and a Bachelor of Arts from Rutgers University.

A retired police officer with 27 years of law-enforcement experience, Jeffrey now serves as a School Resource Officer in Washington Township. He is active in youth coaching and in parish life at the Church of the Holy Family.
