Al is a 41-year resident of Washington Township and a retired Union Ironworker of 50 years. He chairs the Washington Township Planning Board and has given decades to youth coaching, his church, and the Knights of Columbus.

It was Al's service with the 348th General Hospital as a Hospital Corpsman during the Vietnam era that drew him to this work. Having seen first-hand the effects of combat — including on the mentally wounded — Al hopes the Fund will give veterans a space of belonging. "We will let them know they're not alone and people do care."
