John was raised in South Philadelphia and has been a resident of Washington Township for more than 20 years. He comes from a proud military family. His father is an Army veteran, and his uncle is a Vietnam veteran. His wife's grandfather was a Marine veteran and an Iwo Jima survivor. John is very proud of his family's military heritage and the sacrifices they have made in service to our country.

John is a member of the Knights of Columbus Sancta Familia Council, where he serves as the Outside Guard on the Executive Committee. He joined the TriState Veterans Memorial Fund as a way to give back and support veterans and their families in any way he can.

John has been married to his wife for more than 24 years, and together they have two daughters. Their oldest daughter is a college graduate, while their youngest is currently attending college. John has worked as an accountant for more than 30 years and takes great pride in his family, his community, and his commitment to giving back and supporting those who have served our country.
