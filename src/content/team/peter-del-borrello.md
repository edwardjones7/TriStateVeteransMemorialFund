Peter M. Del Borrello III is a dedicated public servant and President of the Council for Washington Township, Gloucester County. Before public life, he built a career in financial services, real estate, and venture capital.

Peter is immensely proud of his family's military heritage — the grandson of two Navy veterans and the nephew of a Vietnam veteran and Purple Heart recipient. That legacy inspires his commitment to supporting veterans and their families.
