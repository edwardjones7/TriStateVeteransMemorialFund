Paul is a lifelong Washington Township resident of 53 years and has been a parishioner of the Church of the Holy Family for 25 years. An active member of the Knights of Columbus Sancta Familia Council, he currently serves as a trustee. Paul has dedicated decades to coaching youth sports, manages one of the parish's two softball teams, and has served as president of his community association for the past 10 years.

Proud of his family's military heritage as the grandson of both Navy and Army World War II veterans, Paul joined the board to support veterans experiencing service-related challenges and to help them access the resources they need to live healthier, more fulfilling lives.
