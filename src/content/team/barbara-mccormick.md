Barbara McCormick, DNP, RN, CEN has over 30 years of nursing experience with Jefferson Health. She earned her doctorate at Drexel University and is an adjunct professor at Thomas Jefferson University.

Commissioned in the U.S. Air Force Reserve Nurse Corps in 1994, Lt. Col. McCormick deployed to Afghanistan and Iraq, and currently serves as Chief Nurse for the 166th Medical Group, Delaware Air National Guard. She has been an American Legion member for over 20 years and has served on the Washington Township Veterans Advisory Board for two decades.
