Jim moved to Washington Township in 1974 and graduated from Washington Township High School. He and his wife Bethann have raised five children and have three grandsons.

Jim joined the Hurffville Fire Company as a volunteer in 1987 and was among the first group of paid firefighters hired in 1999. Promoted to Captain in 2002, he still holds that position, and serves his parish as an usher and Eucharistic Minister.
