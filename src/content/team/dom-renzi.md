Dom has been a proud resident of Washington Township for 46 years. His professional career spanned the banking and account-management industry, where he rose to Vice President of Operations.

A devoted member of the Knights of Columbus — having achieved the 4th Degree, the order's highest rank — Dom is passionate about his work with the Fund. He is dedicated to erasing the stigma surrounding mental health and to ensuring veterans know they are not alone in their struggles.
