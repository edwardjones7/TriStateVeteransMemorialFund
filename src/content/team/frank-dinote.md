Frank has been a parishioner of the Church of the Holy Family and a resident of Washington Township for forty-five years. A member of the Knights of Columbus Sancta Familia Council, he served as Grand Knight and is presently a Trustee of the council.

Retired from Boilermakers Local 28 after forty-one years of service, Frank sits on the board of the TriState Veterans Memorial Fund and truly enjoys working for the betterment of our veterans.
